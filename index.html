Overview

Welcome to Dice & Coin, developed by EditCrafted. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our Android application. Dice & Coin is a casual entertainment app offering coin flip, single dice roll, double dice, triple dice, quad dice, roll statistics, and customizable settings.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not run our own servers and we do not directly collect or store personal information. However, our advertising partner — Google AdMob — may collect certain data as described in this policy.

Short version: We don't collect your name, email, or account data. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and usage signals in order to show you relevant ads.
🗂️

Data We Collect

The app stores the following data locally on your device only and never transmits it to any server:

  • App preferences (sound on/off, vibration on/off, dark mode)
  • Roll and flip history (stored in device memory, cleared on uninstall)

Through Google AdMob, the following data may be collected automatically:

Data Type Purpose Collected By
Advertising ID (GAID) Serve personalized or non-personalized ads; frequency capping Google AdMob
IP address Approximate geolocation for regional ad targeting; fraud prevention Google AdMob
Device information Ad rendering (screen size, OS version, device model, language) Google AdMob
App usage signals Measure ad performance, detect invalid traffic Google AdMob
Cookies & tracking tokens Cross-session ad frequency and measurement Google AdMob
📢

Google AdMob & Advertising

Dice & Coin displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID and other signals to serve ads that are relevant to your interests. The types of ads shown include:

  • Banner ads — displayed at the bottom of certain screens
  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads shown between game actions, no more than once per 60 seconds
  • MREC / inline ads — medium rectangle ads embedded within screen content

No ads are shown on the splash screen. Ads are never placed in a way that obscures game controls or forces interaction. Interstitial ads only appear after gameplay actions and respect a minimum 60-second cooldown between showings.

Opt out of personalized ads: Go to your Android device Settings → Privacy → Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or reset your Advertising ID.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data from apps:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use AdMob

GDPR / EEA / UK Consent

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we are required under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR to obtain your consent before displaying personalized advertisements.

We use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent. When you first launch the app in an EEA or UK region, a consent dialog will appear asking whether you agree to personalized ads.

  • If you consent, AdMob may show personalized ads using your Advertising ID.
  • If you decline, only non-personalized ads will be shown.
  • You can change your choice at any time by tapping Settings → Manage Ad Consent inside the app.
Legal basis: Processing is based on your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
🇺🇸

CCPA – California Users

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with specific rights regarding your personal information.

We do not sell your personal information. However, sharing your Advertising ID with Google AdMob for advertising purposes may qualify as a "sale" or "sharing" under CCPA.

To exercise your California privacy rights, you may:

  • Opt out of sharing for advertising: Go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Limit data use: Use the Manage Ad Consent button in the app's Settings screen to request non-personalized ads only.
  • Request information or deletion: Contact us at the email address below.
⚖️

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of data held about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing — limit how your data is used
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, without penalty

Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, most data requests should be directed to Google. To exercise rights related to Google's data collection, visit Google Account Data & Privacy. To exercise any rights directly with us, use the contact email below.

Children's Privacy

Dice & Coin is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through this app, please contact us immediately at the email address below. We will take prompt steps to delete such information.

The app does not contain content that is inappropriate for minors, but it does display advertisements which may not be child-directed. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise their children's app usage.

🗄️

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data on our servers — because we have no servers. All app data (preferences, roll history) is stored locally on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's data retention policies.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the app and its users. The app communicates with external services only over HTTPS (encrypted connections). We do not transmit your personal preferences or game data over the network.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Google AdMob's security practices are governed by Google's own security policies.
